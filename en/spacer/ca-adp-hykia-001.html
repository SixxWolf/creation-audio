Description

Adapter for installing a pair of 6.5" speakers in many 2006–2021 Hyundai and Kia models: Elantra, Sonata, Tucson, Kona, Forte, Optima, Sportage, Soul and more. It mounts to the vehicle's factory anchor points.

3D-printed in Québec from ABS, a plastic that handles the heat of a cabin in midsummer and the moisture inside doors. Sold in pairs.
