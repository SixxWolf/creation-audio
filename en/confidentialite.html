At Création Audio, we take the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and your rights — in compliance with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

1. Who is responsible

The business responsible for your personal information is:

Création Audio
Business registered in Québec — NEQ 2282510769
Québec City (Québec), Canada — Les Saules area
Email: contact@creationaudio.ca

The person in charge of the protection of personal information at Création Audio can be reached at contact@creationaudio.ca.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what is needed to handle your requests and orders:

  • Contact details : name, email, Messenger ID and, if you provide it, phone number.
  • Order details : products requested, quantities, and the agreed pickup day and time.
  • Billing : the information needed to issue an invoice (name, contact details, possibly address) and the payment method used (cash or Interac e-Transfer).
  • Reseller access request : business name, contact person, email, phone, full address (street address, city, province, postal code) and, if you provide them, website and Facebook page (see below).
  • Waitlist : your email, if you ask to be notified when a sold-out product is back (see below).
  • Customer account (optional): your email, your name and, if you provide it, your phone (see below).
  • Messages : your messages, photos and 3D files you attach (see below).
  • 3D printing or maintenance request : the details of your request, your 3D files, your quote and its tracking (see below).

The site has no online payment. The customer account is optional : without an account, you send us this information yourself, by Messenger or email, when you order.

Customer account

You can create an account to keep your invoices and back-in-stock alerts. In that case:

  • we collect your email (to send you a 6-digit sign-in code — no password), your name and, if you provide it, your phone ;
  • when we link your account to your customer record, you see the history of the invoices we have issued in your name;
  • if you order online, we keep your order (items, note) and its status, and send you a confirmation by email;
  • if you reserve items (72 h), we keep your reservation and the number of reservations not picked up (for the rule “2 not picked up = reservations suspended for 60 days”), and send you a confirmation and a reminder by email;
  • if you choose your pickup (day and time), we keep your choice, and your orders picked up at the same time may be grouped under one pickup and one invoice; we email you a confirmation, change notice or reminder;
  • when an invoice is recorded in your name, we email you a message with the invoice as a PDF, and we keep a copy in our records;
  • if you set your appearance (light, dark, accent color), this choice is saved in your account so it follows you from one device to another;
  • you can edit your contact details and delete your account at any time from the “My account” page;
  • an account with no sign-in for 3 years is deleted automatically.

Deleting your account erases your access, your account details, your alerts, your messages and your uninvoiced service requests. Past invoices and orders stay in our records: the law requires us to keep them (see “Retention”).

Messages

The “Write to us” button opens our messaging. In that case:

  • you confirm your email with a 6-digit code: the conversation is attached to your customer account (created along the way if it didn't exist);
  • we keep your messages, the photos and the 3D files you attach (4 photos and 3 files at most per message), visible to you and us only;
  • we notify you by email when we reply;
  • a conversation is erased 12 months after its last message, and right away if you delete your account.

Prefer not to create an account? Messenger and email are still available.

3D printing and maintenance requests

A request sent from these pages is tied to your customer account. In that case:

  • you confirm your email with a 6-digit code, and we keep your name and, if you provide it, your phone number;
  • we keep the details of your request (part, material, quantity, machines, city or area), your 3D files, the quote, your answer, the date of each step and the discussion of the request, visible only to you and us;
  • we email you the confirmation of your request, the quote and the replies we add to it;
  • the files and photos are removed 12 months after the request ends or is cancelled; the rest of the record stays;
  • if you delete your account, an uninvoiced request is erased; an invoiced request stays in our records with its invoice, without files or discussion.

Reseller access request

If you fill out the form on the “Become a reseller” page, or if you are a dealer:

  • we keep the form's information to review your request and reply, and we email you a confirmation;
  • if your request is approved, your email is used to sign in to the dealer portal (6-digit code, no password) and we keep your dealer record and orders as long as we do business together;
  • if it is declined, it is deleted after 90 days;
  • if you choose delivery on the portal, we keep the delivery address with your order and share it with the carrier to deliver your order.

“Notify me when it's back” waitlist

When a product is sold out, you can leave your email to be notified when it's back. In that case:

  • your email is used only to send you a single email when that product is back in stock — never a newsletter or advertising;
  • it is deleted as soon as the notice is sent, or automatically after 60 days if the product hasn't come back. We then keep only an anonymous record (product requested and date) to know what to reorder;
  • you can ask to be removed from the list at any time by writing to contact@creationaudio.ca.

3. Cookies

The site uses no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. The shop cart only uses your browser's local storage (functional): this data stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you sign in to your account, your login session is also kept in this local storage, on your device, until you sign out. The same goes for your appearance choice, kept on your device to avoid a flash when pages open.

Visit statistics. To know how many people visit the site, we count the pages opened, as simple daily totals: the page, the language, the type of device (mobile or computer) and the general source (Google, Facebook, direct link…). We keep no IP address, no identifier and no profile, and no external analytics service is used. Two markers stay on your device: an indicator of the visit in progress, erased when the tab is closed, and the date of the last day counted. Nothing is counted if your browser sends “Do Not Track”.

4. Use of information

  • Handle, prepare and confirm your orders, pickups and shipments.
  • Communicate with you (questions, quotes, follow-up).
  • Notify you when a product is back in stock, if you asked.
  • Give you access to your account (sign-in by code) and your invoice history.
  • Organize your pickup and email you your confirmations, reminders and invoices.
  • Review your reseller access request and give you access to the dealer portal.
  • Issue invoices and meet our accounting and legal obligations.

We don't use your information for any other purpose without your consent.

5. Hosting and communications

Order, billing, waitlist, messaging and service request data (photos and 3D files included) is stored in our database, hosted by a third-party provider (Supabase) in a data centre located in Montréal (Québec).

Notification emails (back in stock, reply to your message, pickup, service request and quote, PDF invoice) and account sign-in codes are sent through an email delivery service (Resend), whose servers may be located outside Québec (notably in the United States): your address and the content of the notice are sent to it only for the time it takes to send. Our other exchanges may go through Messenger (Meta) and email, under those services' privacy policies, which may also store data outside Québec.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the providers needed to run the service (e.g. hosting or, for a delivered order, the carrier), or if the law requires it.

7. Retention

We keep your information as long as needed for your order and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is destroyed or anonymized. The email left for the waitlist is deleted as soon as the notice is sent, or after 60 days at the latest. A messaging conversation is erased 12 months after its last message. The files and photos of a service request are removed 12 months after it ends or is cancelled. A customer account is deleted on request, or automatically after 3 years without sign-in; invoices are kept for the time required by tax law (6 years).

8. Your rights (Law 25)

You have the right toaccess your information, to have it corrected, and to withdraw your consent. If you have an account, you can correct your details and delete it yourself from the “My account” page. To exercise your other rights, write to us at contact@creationaudio.ca. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI).

9. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your information: restricted access, database-level security rules and an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy: contact@creationaudio.ca.
