Privacy Terms of use Returns & refunds

Since there is no online payment (payment is made at pickup), a “return” is handled after pickup, directly with us. Terms vary by product type.

1. Filament

Filament is sold as final sale : it is non-refundable and non-exchangeable, opened or unopened. It is a consumable whose printing behaviour depends on your equipment and settings, and it is not warranted by Création Audio. Make sure you choose the right material and colour before pickup — we're happy to answer your questions beforehand.

2. Spacers and adapters

Spacers and adapters can be returned or exchanged, no problem, within 14 days of pickup, with proof of purchase (invoice number), the product being in its original condition.

If a spacer has a manufacturing defect (for example a badly formed part), contact us (or by Messenger · email) : we replace it or refund you. Photos and the invoice number help us handle the request quickly.

3. How to make a request

Write to us (or by Messenger, or by email at contact@creationaudio.ca), stating:

  • your invoice number;
  • the product and the reason;
  • photos, if it's a defect.

We'll reply quickly to arrange the exchange, refund or replacement, and pickup of the product if needed.

4. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights granted to you by Québec's Consumer Protection Act, notably for goods that are non-compliant or defective.
