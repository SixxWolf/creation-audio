Questions

What people often ask us.

Which files do you accept?

STL, 3MF, STEP and OBJ, up to 50 MB each and 3 files per request. No file? Describe your part and we'll take it from there.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the material, the print time and the quantity. You get a quote before we start.

What if the date changes?

The date is confirmed by message. If anything changes, we let you know ahead of time.

Can you design the part for me?

Yes. Send a sketch, measurements or a photo of the broken part: we'll propose a 3D model for you to approve before printing.

How do I get my parts?

Pickup in Québec City, or local delivery arranged by message.

Do my files stay private?

Yes. They're only used for your request and are not shared.
