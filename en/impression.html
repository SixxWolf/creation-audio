        Questions

        What people often ask us.

        A question? Write to us or by Messenger · email

        Which files do you accept?

        STL, 3MF, STEP, OBJ and G-code, up to 50 MB each and 3 files per request. The preview works with all of them except STEP. No file? Describe your part and we'll take care of it.

        How much does it cost?

        It depends on weight, print time, material and quantity. You get the price before we start. The weight shown in the preview is an estimate, unless your file is already sliced.

        What if the date changes?

        The date is stated in the quote. If anything changes, we let you know ahead of time.

        Can you design the part for me?

        Yes. Send a sketch, measurements or a photo of the broken part: we'll propose a 3D model for you to approve before printing.

        How do I get my parts?

        Pickup in Québec City, or local delivery arranged by message.

        Do my files stay private?

        Yes. The preview runs in your browser: nothing leaves until you send your request. After that, your files are used only for your request and are not shared.
