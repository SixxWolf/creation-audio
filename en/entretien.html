During the visit

Everything we do on your machines.

  • Build plate

    Cleaned with isopropyl alcohol.

  • Nozzle and purge area

    Residue removed, purge area emptied.

  • Rails and rods

    Cleaned, then lubricated with the right product.

    Carbon rods on the X and P series: alcohol only.

  • Z-axis screws

    Greased with white lithium grease.

  • Fans

    Dusted gently.

  • Exterior and findings

    Wiped down, and anything wearing out is noted for you.

    Questions

    What people often ask us.

    How long does a visit take?

    It depends on the number of machines. When we confirm, we give you an estimated duration.

    Cleaning, lubrication, supplies and a note of what is wearing out. Troubleshooting and parts are extra, with your prior approval.
